Frequently Asked Questions

Key details on engagement structure, attribution transparency, and service integration.

Can we engage InoConnect for a single service or must we adopt the full stack?

You can engage us for individual services such as SEO, PPC Management, or AI Automation. However, our highest-performing partners utilize multiple integrated disciplines where data learnings in paid channels directly inform organic search and content velocity.

How do you verify attribution and prevent double-counting between channels?

We bypass platform-reported vanity metrics by engineering first-party server-side tracking (CAPI) and testing incremental lift against holdout test geos. All reporting is anchored in your CRM and actual bank-cleared revenue.

Who owns the intellectual property and ad accounts created during the partnership?

You retain 100% sovereign ownership of all ad accounts, custom code, landing page repositories, AI model fine-tunes, and attribution models. We never hold your assets or data hostage.

What is the typical ramp-up timeline to see measurable commercial results?

PPC and AI automation workflows yield verifiable efficiency within the first 14 to 30 days. Technical SEO improvements, semantic content rankings, and domain authority typically compound substantially between days 45 and 90.

How does InoConnect integrate with our internal marketing or engineering teams?

We integrate directly into your sprint cycles (Slack, Jira, Linear, or GitHub) functioning as a specialized growth engineering department rather than an external hands-off vendor.
