Knowledge Base

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum budget you manage?

We typically work with clients starting at $5,000/month in ad spend to ensure there is enough data to optimize effectively and see a strong ROI.

How long does it take to see results?

You’ll see traffic immediately. However, the "learning phase" typically takes 2-4 weeks to stabilize, and significant ROAS improvements usually manifest within 60-90 days of continuous optimization.

Do you handle the ad creative (Images/Videos)?

Yes! We have a dedicated creative team that produces ad copy, static images, and short-form video content specifically designed for high conversion.

Which platforms should I start with?

This depends on your audience. B2B often performs best on LinkedIn and Google Search, while B2C/D2C thrives on Meta, TikTok, and Google Shopping. We’ll help you decide in our initial audit.

How do you prevent wasted ad spend?

We use aggressive negative keyword lists, tight geographic targeting, and device-level optimizations to ensure your ads only show to users with the highest probability of converting.

Will I own my ad accounts?

100%. You own the accounts, the data, and the tracking. We are simply the experts managing them for you. You have full transparency at all times.

How do you report on performance?

You get access to a live, 24/7 Looker Studio dashboard that connects directly to your ad accounts. We also have weekly syncs and deep-dive monthly strategy reviews.

Do you help with landing pages?

Yes. Sending paid traffic to a general homepage is usually a mistake. we design and A/B test custom landing pages designed specifically for your ad campaigns.

What is "Server-Side Tracking" and do I need it?

With privacy updates like iOS14, traditional tracking is losing accuracy. We implement server-side tracking to ensure we capture all conversion data, allowing the platform algorithms to optimize better.

Can we pause campaigns at any time?

Yes. While we recommend consistent spending for data stability, you have full control over your budget and can pause campaigns whenever necessary.
