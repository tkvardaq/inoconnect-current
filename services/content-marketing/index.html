Knowledge Base

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

How often should we be publishing content?

Quality beats quantity. We typically recommend 2 to 4 high-value pieces per month, supplemented by daily social micro-content.

Do you help with content distribution?

Yes. A great article is useless if no one reads it. We assist with social promotion, email distribution, and strategic outreach.

Can you write for technical/specialized industries?

Absolutely. We have a network of specialized writers across SaaS, Finance, Healthcare, and Tech to ensure technical accuracy.

How do you measure the ROI of content marketing?

We track organic traffic growth, lead volume from content pages, and "assisted conversions" where content plays a role in the sales journey.

Do we own the rights to the content?

Yes. Once a piece is finalized and paid for, you own 100% of the intellectual property rights to that content forever.

How do you choose topics to write about?

We combine SEO data (what people are searching for) with Sales data (what your customers are asking your sales team).

Does content marketing help with SEO?

Content is the #1 driver of SEO. Without high-quality, relevant content, it is nearly impossible to rank for competitive keywords.

What is "Gated Content"?

It's high-value content (like a guide) that users can access in exchange for their email address—a primary driver of lead generation.

How long does it take to see results from content?

Content builds compounded value. You'll see minor impacts in 2-3 months, with major breakthroughs typically occurring between months 6 and 12.

Do you help with social media content as well?

Yes. We repurpose our long-form content into social-ready formats for LinkedIn, Twitter, and Instagram to maximize reach.
