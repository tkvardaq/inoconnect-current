Knowledge Base

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

Which social platforms should my business be on?

It depends on your audience. We help you identify where your ideal customers spend their time and focus our efforts there for maximum ROI.

Do you respond to comments and messages?

Yes. We offer full community management, ensuring every comment and message is handled professionally and promptly.

Do we need to provide our own photos and videos?

We can work with what you have, or our creative team can produce high-quality assets for you, including graphic design and video editing.

How often do you post?

Posting frequency varies by platform and strategy. We typically recommend 3 to 5 high-quality posts per week per platform.

How do you measure success on social media?

We look at Engagement Rate, Follower Growth, and most importantly, Click-Through Rate and Social-Sourced Conversions.

Can you help with influencer marketing?

Yes. We can help identify, vet, and manage relationships with influencers who align with your brand values.

How long does it take to see growth?

Organic social growth is a slow burn. You'll see engagement spikes within 30 days, while significant follower growth usually takes 3-6 months.

Do you manage paid social ads as well?

Yes, but that is covered under our PPC services. We recommend a mix of organic management and paid amplification for best results.

Will you use my brand's voice?

Absolutely. Our first step is a deep dive into your brand guidelines and persona to ensure our posts sound identical to your internal team.

Can we review posts before they go live?

Yes. You'll have access to our content calendar where you can review, suggest edits, and approve all content before it is scheduled.
