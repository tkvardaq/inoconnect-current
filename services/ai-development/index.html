Technical FAQs

Key answers regarding our AI development tech stack and model ownership.

Do we own the custom fine-tuned weights and codebase?

Yes. All fine-tuned LoRA adapters, dataset curation scripts, agent orchestration code, and deployment manifests belong 100% to your organization.

Which foundation models do you recommend building on?

We evaluate cost, latency, and reasoning capabilities: OpenAI GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet for high-complexity planning; fine-tuned Llama 3 and Mistral for high-throughput, private on-premise inference.

Yes. We regularly deploy complete AI architectures inside customer-owned VPCs using Terraform, AWS Bedrock, or Google Vertex AI.
