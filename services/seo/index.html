Knowledge Base

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take to produce results?

SEO is a long-term play. While technical fixes can show impact in weeks, significant organic growth typically takes 4-9 months depending on your market's competitiveness.

Do you guarantee 1 rankings?

No ethical agency can guarantee a specific rank on Google. However, we guarantee we will implement the best-in-class strategies that give you the highest possible probability of ranking.

Will I need to change my website?

Yes, usually. SEO requires technical and content changes. We work closely with your team (or handle it ourselves) to ensure your site meets Google's standards.

How do you choose which keywords to target?

We prioritize keywords based on three factors: Search Volume, Keyword Difficulty, and Business Value (how likely the user is to convert).

Do you write the content for us?

Yes. We have a team of expert writers and editors who specialize in producing SEO-optimized content that actually sounds human and professional.

What are backlinks and why are they important?

Think of a backlink as a "vote of confidence" from another site. Google sees quality links as a signal that your site is an authority in its niche.

Is SEO better than PPC?

They serve different purposes. PPC is instant but expensive. SEO takes time but builds long-term equity. Most successful brands use both in tandem.

What is Technical SEO?

It's the "under the hood" work—site speed, mobile responsiveness, XML sitemaps, and SSL—that ensures search engines can easily navigate your site.

How do you measure SEO success?

We track rankings, but we care more about Organic Traffic, Lead Volume, and Organic Conversion Rate. You'll see all of this in your custom dashboard.

What happens if Google updates its algorithm?

We build your SEO on "First Principles" (quality content, good UX, real authority). This makes your site resilient to most algorithm changes.
