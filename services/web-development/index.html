Knowledge Base

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom website cost?

Pricing depends on complexity. A custom lead-gen site starts at $5,000, while complex e-commerce or portal builds vary based on features.

How long does it take to build a site?

A typical custom build takes 4 to 10 weeks from discovery to launch, depending on the scope and your team's feedback speed.

Will I be able to update content myself?

Yes. We build every site with an easy-to-use CMS (like Contentful or Sanity) so you can update text and images without needing a developer.

Are your websites mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. We use a "Mobile-First" approach, ensuring the experience is perfect on phones, tablets, and desktop monitors.

Do you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. We handle the technical setup of your hosting (Vercel, Netlify, or AWS) and ensure your domain is configured correctly.

What is a "headless" website?

It's a modern architecture that separates the "head" (visuals) from the "body" (CMS), resulting in much faster speeds and better security.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes. We offer support packages that include security updates, speed optimizations, and monthly content changes.

Can you help fix my existing website?

We prefer building from scratch to ensure quality, but we do offer "Website Audits" to help improve your current site's performance.

Will the site be optimized for SEO?

Yes. Technical SEO is baked into our development process, including meta tags, site structure, and fast load times.

What if I need a custom feature or app?

Our team consists of full-stack engineers who can build custom functionality, APIs, and web applications tailored to your needs.
