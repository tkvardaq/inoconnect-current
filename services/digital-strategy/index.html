Knowledge Base

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

How often is the digital strategy updated?

We build 12-month roadmaps that are formally reviewed every quarter and tactically adjusted every month based on performance data.

Do we need to have a large team for this?

No. We tailor our strategy to your available resources—whether you have an internal team or are looking for us to handle execution.

What is the ROI of a digital strategy?

Strategy reduces wasted spend and increases conversion rates. Most clients see a significant ROI through efficiency gains alone within the first 6 months.

Do you help with software selection?

Yes. Part of our strategy service is auditing your MarTech stack and recommending the best tools for your specific needs and budget.

Can you work with our existing agency?

Yes. We often act as the "Fractional CMO" or Strategic Lead, coordinating between internal teams and external specialist agencies.

How long does the initial discovery take?

The deep discovery phase typically takes 3 to 4 weeks, after which we present your finalized Growth Blueprint.

Is this service just for digital, or also offline?

Our primary focus is digital, but we ensure yours is integrated with your offline sales processes and brand touchpoints.

How do you measure a strategy's success?

Success is measured by the achievement of your "North Star Metric"—which is typically Revenue Growth or Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reduction.

Do you provide competitor analysis?

Yes. A major part of our strategy is a deep-dive into your competitors' SEO, PPC, and Social strategies to find their weaknesses.

What is a "Fractional CMO"?

It's a senior-level marketing executive who works for your company part-time, providing high-level strategy without the cost of a full-time hire.
