Frequently Asked Questions

Key considerations when deploying autonomous AI systems.

How do autonomous agents prevent hallucinations during customer interactions?

Our agents utilize grounded retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and strict output schema validation. If confidence falls below 95%, the request is gracefully escalated to a human operator.

Which CRMs and tools can your AI agents integrate with?

We natively connect agents to HubSpot, Salesforce, Close, Slack, Jira, Linear, Postgres, and custom webhooks via secure REST and GraphQL endpoints.

What is the ongoing maintenance required once agents are in production?

We provide automated uptime telemetry, model drift monitoring, and prompt updates as foundation model APIs evolve.
