Quantitative Foundations

The Mathematical Formulas Behind Our Models

We believe in radical transparency. Every calculator on this platform is powered by industry-standard mathematical definitions used by private equity and enterprise growth teams.

Model 01 • ROAS & MER

Marketing Efficiency Ratio

MER = Total Revenue / Total Ad Spend

Unlike in-platform ROAS which suffers from attribution overlap, MER measures true top-line business impact across all paid channels combined.

Model 02 • Conversion Lift

CRO Revenue Sensitivity

ΔRev = Traffic × ΔCR × AOV

Models the exact dollar value gained from incremental conversion lift without spending an extra cent on paid media acquisition.

Model 03 • Customer Value

Gross Margin LTV

LTV = (ARPU × GM%) / Churn Rate

Calculates true gross profit lifetime value to identify the maximum allowable customer acquisition cost (CAC) for sustainable scale.

Model 04 • Lifecycle

Email Attribution Yield

Yield = Subs × OR × CTR × CVR × AOV

Simulates revenue potential across welcome sequences, cart abandonments, and automated post-purchase win-back campaigns.
