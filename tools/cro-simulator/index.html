Funnel Economics

Why CRO Outperforms Paid Ad Scaling

Doubling your traffic doubles your advertising spend. Doubling your conversion rate doubles your revenue with zero additional advertising cost.

01

Incremental Orders Formula

Δ Orders = Traffic × (Target CR% - Base CR%)

Measures direct expansion in transaction count without paying search engines or social algorithms for new clicks.

02

Direct Revenue Expansion

Δ Monthly Revenue = Δ Orders × AOV

Every additional converted customer directly enters pure contribution margin because customer acquisition cost on existing traffic is zero.

03

Effective CAC Deflation

New CAC = Ad Spend / Total Converted Customers

As total conversions expand across the exact same ad spend, your blended customer acquisition cost compresses proportionally.

Frequently Asked Questions

Statistical significance, testing methodology, and implementation requirements.

How much monthly traffic is required to run statistically valid A/B tests?

To reach a 95% statistical confidence interval within 2-4 weeks, we recommend at least 15,000 unique visitors per variant and a minimum of 200 conversions per branch. For lower-traffic sites, we apply qualitative user testing, heatmaps, and high-impact heuristic redesigns.

What is the difference between heuristic CRO and client-side A/B testing?

Heuristic CRO audits the cognitive load, value proposition clarity, and usability friction based on established human-computer interaction principles. Client-side A/B testing splits live visitor traffic between variants to empirically validate which hypothesis generates higher revenue.

Can CRO harm existing SEO search rankings?

No, when executed following Google's testing guidelines. We implement canonical tags to the control page, utilize server-side edge redirects to prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS), and maintain semantic HTML headings and keyword relevance throughout all test variants.

How does page load speed impact conversion rate?

Empirical studies demonstrate that every 100ms delay in page load time reduces conversions by up to 7%. Sites loading in under 1 second convert at 2.5x the rate of sites that take 5+ seconds to render interactively.
