Mathematical Transparency

How This Model Works

We use the standard institutional unit-economics formula utilized by venture capital firms and software CFOs.

// Core LTV Formula
LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin %) ÷ Monthly Churn Rate %

• ARPU (Average Revenue Per User): The monthly recurring revenue (MRR) generated per active subscription account.

• Gross Margin %: Direct delivery margin after cloud hosting (AWS/GCP), customer support infrastructure, and API COGS.

• Monthly Churn Rate %: The percentage of customers who cancel or downgrade per 30-day cohort cycle.

Clarity

LTV:CAC Modeler FAQs

Why is Gross Margin factored into LTV?

Calculating LTV purely on top-line revenue leads to severe overspending. Only the gross margin dollars are available to cover operating overheads, customer acquisition cost, and net profit.

What is the golden ratio for SaaS LTV:CAC?

A 3:1 ratio is generally considered the baseline threshold for sustainable venture-backed SaaS. Top-quartile bootstrapped or capital-efficient firms target 4.5:1 or higher.
