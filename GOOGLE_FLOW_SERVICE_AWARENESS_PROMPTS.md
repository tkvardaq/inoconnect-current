# Google Flow (Veo / VideoFX) Master Prompts
## Public Service Awareness Hero Loop (20s Seamless Showreel)

**Target Aesthetic:** High-End Documentary Cinema (HBO / Apple TV style)  
**Tone:** Empathetic, Grounded, Reassuring, Human-Centric (Zero aggressive sales hype)  
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Landscape (or 9:16 if using vertical Flow, which we will crop/frame)  
**Framerate:** 24fps / 30fps  

---

### Why This Narrative Works (Psychological Architecture)
```
Beat 1 (0-5s): THE SEARCH      -> Solitary reflection, seeking clarity amidst noise (Lowers sales resistance)
Beat 2 (5-10s): THE CONNECTION  -> Compassionate, active listening & validation (Triggers emotional relief)
Beat 3 (10-15s): THE SERVICE   -> Calm, dependable collaboration & craft (Builds psychological safety)
Beat 4 (15-20s): THE IMPACT    -> Quiet confidence, freedom, human flourishing (Peak-end elevation)
Loop Seam: Beat 4 twilight horizon flows seamlessly back into Beat 1 dusk window (Imperceptible loop)
```

---

### Scene 1: THE SEARCH — Seeking What Matters (0:00 – 0:05)
* **Emotional State:** Thoughtful contemplation, longing for truth amidst superficial noise.
* **Camera Movement:** Very slow cinematic push-in (dolly in).
* **Copy-Paste Google Flow Prompt:**
```text
Cinematic 16:9 medium slow tracking shot of a thoughtful young professional standing beside a vast panoramic floor-to-ceiling glass window of a modern architectural gallery at blue-hour dusk. Outside, the soft blurred city twilight lights begin to glow in deep navy and amber. The person pauses with a calm, searching expression, glancing thoughtfully away from a glowing minimalist smartphone toward the horizon. Subtle gentle breathing, natural hair movement, soft ambient reflections in the polished glass. Photorealistic, 35mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, serene documentary cinema grade, peaceful contemplative atmosphere.
```
* **Negative Prompt:**
```text
cartoon, 3d render, cgi, exaggerated smiling, aggressive motion, jitter, flicker, oversaturated, morphing, watermark, text, low resolution
```
