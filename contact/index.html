What access is required for the initial growth audit?

Read-only viewer access to your Google Analytics 4 (GA4) property, existing ad accounts (Google Ads, Meta, or LinkedIn), and high-level conversion numbers. We do not require admin credentials or edit permissions.

Sprint work begins within 3 to 5 business days of contract execution. We immediately stand up your dedicated communication channels, audit current CAPI data streams, and schedule our technical discovery workshop.
