Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our growth engineering methodologies, technical attribution systems, contract terms, and onboarding process.

How does InoConnect track revenue accurately in a post-cookie and iOS 14.5+ environment? ▼
We deploy custom server-side Conversions API (CAPI) infrastructure integrated directly with your backend database, Shopify/Stripe, or CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot). By bypassing browser cookie limitations, we achieve 98%+ attribution signal fidelity, allowing ad algorithms on Google and Meta to optimize against true closed-won gross profit rather than superficial clicks.
What makes your AI automation different from off-the-shelf chatbot wrappers? ▼
Our AI systems are autonomous operational pipelines built with custom Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), vector databases, and real-time webhook routing. They ingest new leads in under 2.4 seconds, perform multi-point qualification against your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), enrich CRM data via Clearbit/Apollo, and automatically schedule high-priority demos on your sales team's calendar.
Do we retain 100% ownership of our advertising accounts, code, and landing pages? ▼
Yes, absolutely. We believe agency asset hostage-taking is predatory. Every Google Ads account, Meta Business Manager, analytics container, custom script, and Figma design file is created directly within your corporate accounts or transferred to you with 100% intellectual property sovereignty from day one.
How quickly can our business expect to see tangible commercial impact? ▼
Immediate quick-win improvements in conversion rate and paid acquisition efficiency typically materialize within the first 14 to 30 days. Full compounding effects—such as programmatic organic search rankings, AI-driven lead nurturing pipelines, and multi-channel scale—hit maximum velocity around days 45 through 90.
How does InoConnect collaborate with our existing in-house marketing team? ▼
We act as a high-velocity specialized force multiplier. If you have an internal brand team or marketing director, we handle the deep technical search architecture, server-side data pipelines, programmatic ad creative scaling, and AI automation. We embed seamlessly into your Slack or Teams environment with weekly sprint reports.
What does your pricing structure and engagement model look like? ▼
We provide transparent, deliverable-backed pricing tied directly to strategic scope and performance milestones. We don't trap clients in 12-month lock-in contracts; our work earns its place every quarter through verifiable pipeline and unit economic ROI.
