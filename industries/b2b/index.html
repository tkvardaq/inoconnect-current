Knowledge Base

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

How do you define a "Qualified Lead"?

We work with your sales team to define the criteria—typically a mix of company size, job title, and specific intent markers.

Do you help with the sales process?

We specialize in Sales Enablement—providing the content and tools your sales team needs to move leads from "Interested" to "Closed."

How long is a typical B2B growth project?

Because of long sales cycles, we typically work with B2B clients on 6-12 month engagements to see the full revenue impact.

Can you help us clean up our CRM data?

Yes. Part of our onboarding is ensuring your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.) is correctly configured to track marketing performance.

What is the best B2B social platform?

LinkedIn is usually the primary channel, but we also find high ROI on niche industry forums and even YouTube for educational content.

How do you handle "Executive Outreach"?

Through Account-Based Marketing (ABM)—building highly personal experiences and content for specific high-value individuals.

Do you write our whitepapers?

Yes. We have expert B2B copywriters who can interview your technical team and produce professional, deep-dive assets.

How do you measure B2B marketing success?

We track MQLs (Marketing Qualified Leads), SQLs (Sales Qualified Leads), and ultimately, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) and Pipeline Value.

Can you help with trade shows?

Yes. We can integrate your digital marketing (pre-show ads, post-show follow-up) with your physical presence at industry events.

What is "Intent Data"?

It's data that shows which companies are currently researching topics related to your services, allowing us to target them before they even visit your site.
