Knowledge Base

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with both traditional and online institutions?

Yes. We specialize in growth for both physical campuses and modern, purely digital E-learning platforms.

Can you help us reach international students?

Absolutely. We have experience managing global campaigns across multiple continents and languages to drive international enrollment.

How do you track if a lead actually enrolls?

We integrate our marketing tracking directly with your CRM or student management system to see the full journey from click to enrollment.

Do you help with content for our courses?

While we focus on marketing content (landing pages, ads, guides), we can advise on your course structure to improve student retention and LTV.

What is the best platform for reaching students?

For Gen-Z, TikTok and Instagram are dominant. For professional/corporate students, LinkedIn and Google Search provide the highest ROI.

How long until we see an increase in enrollments?

Paid campaigns can drive leads in the first week. Full funnel optimization and organic SEO usually take 3 to 6 months for significant impact.

Do you offer fractional marketing leadership for schools?

Yes. For many institutions, we act as their Fractional Head of Growth, providing high-level strategy and coordinating their internal teams.

Can you help us lower our cost-per-lead?

Yes. By optimizing your ad targeting and improving your website's conversion rate, we can significantly lower your acquisition costs.

Do you handle the creative and design for our ads?

Yes. Our creative team produces all ad copy, graphics, and video content specifically designed for the education sector.

Will we own our ad accounts and data?

100%. You own all accounts and data. We simply manage them for you with full transparency through your custom dashboard.
