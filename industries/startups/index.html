Knowledge Base

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

When is the right time for a startup to hire a growth agency?

As soon as you have a stable MVP (Minimum Viable Product). We help you find the users you need to validate your product assumptions.

Do you work in exchange for equity?

We typically work on a cash-fee basis, but we are open to hybrid models for highly promising startups with strong backing.

How fast can you launch our first experiments?

We can typically have your tracking live and first ad experiments running within 7 to 10 days of our kickoff call.

Can you help us prepare for a funding round?

Yes. We focus on the metrics that investors care about: CAC, LTV, Retention, and MoM Revenue growth.

Do you handle the creative and copy?

Yes. Startups move fast, and so do we. We handle all design, copy, and video production for our growth experiments.

What if our product changes mid-campaign?

That's the startup life. We are built for agility. We can pivot your entire marketing strategy in 24 hours to match your new product direction.

How much budget do we need for testing?

We typically recommend a "Testing Budget" of $2k - $5k for the first month to gather enough data for significant insights.

Do you help with organic growth or just paid?

Both. We look for the most efficient path. Often that’s a mix of clever organic loops and targeted, high-tempo paid media.

Will you train our internal team?

Yes. We document all our winning playbooks so that when you're ready to bring growth in-house, your team can hit the ground running.

How often do we sync?

We have weekly "Sprint Reviews" where we analyze the past week’s data and decide on the next week’s experiments.
