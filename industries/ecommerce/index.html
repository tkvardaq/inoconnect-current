Knowledge Base

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

Which e-commerce platforms do you support?

We are specialists in Shopify, Magento, and BigCommerce, but we can manage marketing for any stable e-commerce platform.

Do you help with product photography?

We can help coordinate photography and UGC (User Generated Content) creators to ensure your ad creative is top-tier.

How do you handle ad creative for many products?

We focus our primary acquisition on your "Star Products" (best sellers) while using dynamic ads to retarget users with specific items they viewed.

What is a good ROAS?

It depends on your margins. Typically, we look for a 3x to 5x ROAS for acquisition and much higher for retargeting and email.

Can you help lower my cart abandonment?

Yes. Through a combination of checkout UX improvements and automated Email/SMS follow-up flows.

Do you manage Amazon Ads?

We focus primarily on your own "Direct" website (D2C), but we can coordinate with your Amazon team for a unified brand voice.

How do you track where sales come from?

We use a mix of GA4, platform pixels, and third-party attribution tools like Triple Whale to get the most accurate picture possible.

How long until we see a jump in sales?

Paid media can drive sales in the first 24 hours. Full funnel optimization and retention gains typically manifest over 30 to 90 days.

Do we need to spend a lot on emails?

Email marketing (Klaviyo) is one of the highest ROI channels. Once set up, it essentially generates "free" revenue from your existing list.

Do you help with international expansion?

Yes. We can help you navigate multi-currency, multi-language marketing to reach customers in new global markets.
