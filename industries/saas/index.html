Knowledge Base

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with B2B or B2C SaaS?

We specialize in both. Our strategies focus on the specific psychology of your target user, whether it’s a single consumer or an enterprise decision-maker.

Can you help lower our CAC?

Yes. We do this by optimizing your ad targeting, improving landing page conversion rates, and building an organic SEO moat that delivers lower-cost leads over time.

Do you handle technical integrations?

Yes. Our engineering team can help set up tracking pixels, API connections, and data dashboards to ensure your marketing and product data are perfectly aligned.

How do you help reduce churn?

We implement "Retention Marketing"—using email, SMS, and in-app triggers to re-engage users who are showing signs of inactivity.

Do you help with Product Hunt launches?

Yes. We can coordinate full launch campaigns, including outreach and creative assets, to give your product the best possible start.

What is a "North Star Metric"?

It's the one key metric that best expresses the core value that your product delivers to customers. We help you identify and optimize for it.

Can you help with Account-Based Marketing (ABM)?

Absolutely. For enterprise SaaS, we create highly personalized campaigns targeting specific high-value accounts and their key decision-makers.

How long until we see MRR growth?

You’ll see leading indicators (signups/trials) within weeks. Compounded MRR growth typically becomes significant within 3 to 6 months.

Do we need to change our product for your marketing to work?

Sometimes. We may suggest UI/UX changes to your onboarding or trial flow to improve conversion rates and user retention.

Do you offer fractional growth leadership?

Yes. For many SaaS clients, we act as their Fractional Head of Growth, providing strategic direction alongside our execution services.
